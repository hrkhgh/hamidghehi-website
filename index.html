My Commitment

Why I’m Running

Richmond Hill is growing, and Ward 2 deserves a strong, accessible and accountable voice at City Hall.

I am running to help protect the quality of our neighbourhoods, address traffic and safety concerns, encourage responsible development, and ensure municipal tax dollars are managed carefully.

Most importantly, residents should feel heard before important decisions affecting their neighbourhoods are made.

“Good local government starts by listening to the people it serves.”
Meet the Candidate

Meet Hamidreza

Hamidreza Kheirkhah Ghehi is committed to serving Richmond Hill residents through responsible, transparent and community-focused municipal leadership.

His campaign is built around a simple principle: residents deserve to know how decisions are made, how their tax dollars are spent, and how growth will affect their neighbourhoods.

As a Candidate for Ward 2 City Councillor, Richmond Hill, Hamidreza wants to bring residents, businesses, community organizations and the City together to find practical solutions to local challenges.

Working for Ward 2

Priorities for Our Community

Practical local priorities focused on protecting neighbourhood quality, improving daily life, and ensuring residents receive value from their municipal government.

Responsible Growth

Support responsible development while protecting established neighbourhoods and ensuring roads, parks, transit and public services keep pace with population growth.

Traffic & Road Safety

Advocate for safer residential streets, improved traffic management and practical solutions to congestion affecting Ward 2 families.

Safe Neighbourhoods

Support community safety initiatives and stronger coordination between residents, municipal services and community partners.

Responsible Tax Spending

Promote fiscal discipline, scrutinize municipal spending and focus tax dollars on services and infrastructure that deliver real value to residents.

Parks & Community Spaces

Protect and improve parks, recreation facilities and public spaces that contribute to healthy, connected and family-friendly neighbourhoods.

Transparent City Hall

Support clear communication, accessible representation and transparent decision-making so residents understand how and why important decisions are made.

A Strong Voice for Ward 2

Ward 2 is home to diverse neighbourhoods, families, businesses and community organizations that all contribute to Richmond Hill’s success.

As our city grows, Ward 2 needs representation focused on both today’s concerns and the long-term quality of our neighbourhoods.

  • Listening to neighbourhood concerns
  • Protecting community character
  • Planning responsibly for future growth
  • Improving communication between City Hall and residents
