Working for Ward 2

Priorities for Our Community

Practical local priorities focused on protecting neighbourhood quality, improving daily life, supporting local businesses, and ensuring taxpayers receive measurable value.

Top Priority

Traffic & Road Safety

Advocate for safer residential streets, traffic-calming near school zones, data-driven intersection timing, and active collaboration with York Region to reduce congestion.

Top Priority

Responsible Growth

Ensure infrastructure (roads, parks, schools, transit) keeps pace with population growth while protecting established neighbourhood character and housing diversity.

Top Priority

Local Business Support

Establish a single point-of-contact concierge for small businesses, streamline municipal permits, and partner with the Board of Trade to foster local jobs.

Safe & Connected Neighbourhoods

Strengthen community safety initiatives through evidence-based coordination between residents, municipal services, and York Regional Police.

Parks & Community Spaces

Protect and upgrade existing parks, green trails, and recreation facilities to support healthy, connected, and family-friendly Ward 2 neighborhoods.

Transparent & Fiscal Discipline

Maintain strict oversight on the city's 28% portion of property tax, scrutinize spending for real ROI, and make decision-making clear and accessible.

48-Hour Resident Response Standard

When you contact our office with a Ward 2 concern, you will receive a direct update identifying the exact department and person responsible for follow-up within 48 hours.

Ward 2 Promise & Progress Tracker

Real accountability means measurable results. We will publish a publicly accessible tracker mapping key local issues, action items, target deadlines, and completed outcomes.

Our Community

A Strong Voice for Ward 2

Ward 2 is home to diverse neighbourhoods, families, local main street businesses, and community organizations that all contribute to Richmond Hill’s success.

As our city grows, Ward 2 needs representation focused on both today’s daily traffic and safety concerns and the long-term quality of our neighbourhoods.

  • Listening to neighbourhood concerns with dedicated response standards
  • Protecting community character while managing infrastructure growth
  • Supporting local commercial hubs and small business entrepreneurs
  • Championing inclusive community events that bring diverse groups together
Meet the Candidate

Meet Hamidreza

Hamidreza Kheirkhah Ghehi is committed to serving Richmond Hill residents through responsible, transparent, and outcome-oriented municipal leadership.

His campaign is built around a simple principle: residents deserve to know how decisions are made, how tax dollars are spent, and how growth will affect their daily commute and quality of life.

As a Candidate for Ward 2 City Councillor, Hamidreza brings a practical problem-solving approach to ensure City Hall delivers real, measurable results for our community.
