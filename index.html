Why I’m Running

Richmond Hill is growing, and Ward 2 deserves a strong, accessible and accountable voice at City Hall.

I am running to help protect the quality of our neighbourhoods, address traffic and safety concerns, encourage responsible development, and ensure municipal tax dollars are managed carefully.
